# Shader credits

## Ember

The flame effect is adapted from [trinketMage's Shadertoy shader
3tcBzH](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/3tcBzH), also attributed in the
[three.js Shadertoy example](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/master/examples/webgpu_shadertoy.html).

The original CC-BY-NC-SA licensing notice is retained in `shaders/ember.frag`
and in the embedded release-pack source. The repository's GPL-3.0-only license
does not replace those upstream terms. The Forge adaptation adds glass clipping,
theme colors, blur, background opacity and effect controls, and changes the
noise implementation and compositing for the COSMIC renderer.
